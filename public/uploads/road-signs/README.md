# Ontario road sign images

These SVG road sign assets are local copies of public-domain Ontario road sign files from Wikimedia Commons.

Wikimedia Commons metadata for the selected files reported:

- License: Public domain
- Artist/source: Government of Ontario / Ministry of Transportation or Government of Ontario

The app stores local copies so learner practice works offline/local-first and does not hotlink Commons images.
